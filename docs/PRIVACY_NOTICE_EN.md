This Notice explains personal-information handling related to Overout distribution, purchases, support, Security, the Public Repository, and other related operations.

Overout is a Product created through `Operation: Protocol-Overout` within `Project: Code-NOAH`. This Notice clarifies Overout-specific product behavior, sales, distribution, and support under common GeneSIS / Project: Code-NOAH privacy operations; it does not establish a separate controller.

## 1. Controller / Official Contact

- **Published / Operated By:** GeneSIS
- **Personal Information Controller:** GeneSIS-Operator
- **Official Contact:** `genesis.official.poc@mail.com`

Final decisions concerning personal-information handling are made by the GeneSIS-Operator.

## 2. Local-first Product Data

The following local Overout data is not automatically collected or transmitted to GeneSIS merely through normal use:

- Text salvaged from the Windows Clipboard
- Shared Document Buffer
- Content edited or organized in SALVAGE / PREPARATION
- Tags / Layer Markers / Timestamps
- Local files produced through File Output
- Local file paths
- `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Overout\config.json`
- Other local user data

Overout v1.0.0 does not require network functionality to send Clipboard contents to GeneSIS or an external service.

## 3. Information GeneSIS May Handle

GeneSIS primarily handles information actually displayed to it by a Platform or voluntarily submitted by a user.

### 3.1 Support / Security

This may include name / display name / handle, email, inquiry text, attachments, Overout and Windows versions, OS / runtime information, logs, screenshots, reproduction information, submitted configuration, and handling history.

Redact unnecessary personal information, credentials, secrets, confidential Clipboard Text, and third-party information.

### 3.2 BOOTH / Purchase / Founder Support

GeneSIS may handle information actually visible through BOOTH or submitted by the purchaser, including:

- Purchaser information visible to GeneSIS
- Order / Product / Purchase Time records
- Information required for Overout purchase verification
- Project: Code-NOAH Phase 1 Founder Support eligibility
- Information required to provide the Overout Additional Deliverable

GeneSIS does not directly process BOOTH payments and does not seek complete card information.

### 3.3 GitHub

This may include Public Profile information, Issues / Comments, Documentation / Development Feedback, Security Reports, Private Vulnerability Reporting submissions, and other information the user publicly posts or sends through GitHub.

### 3.4 Other Official Media

Public activity on note, X, and YouTube may be observed. Social-media DMs are not formal support routes.

### 3.5 Technical Records

Email and Platform providers may independently retain technical records for communication, Security, and operation. GeneSIS does not obtain or control all information held by those providers.

## 4. Purposes of Use

Information may be used for:

- Inquiry review and response
- Defect / compatibility / Security investigation
- Documentation improvement
- Release / Known Limitations / Support maintenance
- Purchase verification
- Founder Support eligibility and Overout Additional Deliverable provision
- EULA / rights / terms handling
- Privacy and rights requests
- Abuse / spam / threat / harassment / Security-risk response
- Legal compliance
- GeneSIS / Project: Code-NOAH development and operational records

## 5. AI Assistance

The GeneSIS-Operator may use AI to assist Documentation, translation, technical review, or organization of inquiries.

Private information is reduced, anonymized, or redacted where appropriate.

Passwords, authentication codes, private keys, and complete payment information are not intentionally submitted to AI.

AI is not the sole final decision-maker for purchase / Founder eligibility, rights, Privacy requests, or Security handling. Final decisions are made by the GeneSIS-Operator.

## 7. Retention / Deletion

No single fixed retention period applies to all information.

Information is retained as reasonably necessary for the purpose, handling records, disputes, abuse / Security response, Platform operation, or applicable law.

Information no longer needed is deleted or made less identifiable where reasonably possible. Information retained by third-party Platforms remains subject to their own functions and procedures.

## 8. Safeguards

Considering the nature of the information and the scale of individual operation, measures may include:

- Appropriate account passwords
- Two-factor authentication where available
- Limiting accounts, devices, and connected applications with access
- Avoiding unnecessary collection of credentials or sensitive information
- Separating public and non-public information
- Organizing and deleting information no longer needed
- Appropriate action when a Security issue is identified

Absolute Security cannot be guaranteed on the Internet or third-party Platforms.

## 9. Access, Correction and Other Privacy Requests

To the extent provided by applicable law, an individual may request notice of purpose, access, correction, addition, deletion, suspension of use, erasure, or suspension of third-party provision concerning information about that individual.

**Privacy Request Contact**

`genesis.official.poc@mail.com`

Suggested subject:

```text

```

Identify the information concerned and the requested action where reasonably possible.

Minimum identity confirmation may be requested to protect third-party rights, Security, and prevent fraudulent requests.

A request may be denied in whole or in part when required or permitted by law, when it would harm another person's rights or safety, when GeneSIS does not hold the information, or when GeneSIS lacks authority to modify information held by a Platform.

See [Contact and Support](./CONTACT_AND_SUPPORT_EN.md) for general contact instructions.
